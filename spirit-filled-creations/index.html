Our Foundation

Filled with the Spirit of God

“and he has filled him with the Spirit of God, with wisdom, with understanding, with knowledge and with all kinds of skills—” — Exodus 35:31

In the book of Exodus, God filled Bezalel with His Spirit so that he could create beautiful and skillful work for the Tabernacle. This verse reminds us that creativity, craftsmanship, and artistic skill are gifts from God.

Every painting, key chain, and fridge magnet in this shop is made with that same understanding: that the ability to create is a gift, and that the work of our hands can bring beauty, encouragement, and joy into everyday spaces.

Exodus 35:31

About the Artist

Handmade, one piece at a time.

Welcome to Spirit-Filled Creations. I create original paintings, handcrafted key chains, and unique fridge magnets from my studio. Each item is made slowly and intentionally — no mass production, no shortcuts.

Whether it’s a larger canvas meant to become a focal point in a home, or a small magnet that brings a quiet smile every time the fridge is opened, my hope is that these pieces carry a sense of care and beauty into the places they go.

Shipping is available across India. Custom orders and special requests are welcome — just reach out.
